Better Torsh: Flashlight & Torch

A simple, bright flashlight for Android. One tap turns on the LED torch. It also has a one-tap SOS button, a screen light, patterns and a light that follows your music. No ads, no internet, no tracking.

Flashlight and screen light

  • LED torch with adjustable brightness on phones that support it (Android 13+)
  • Screen light in white, a warm reading light or a red night light
  • Flashlight widget, quick-settings tile and lock-screen access
  • Shake to turn the flashlight on, with a pocket guard

SOS, strobe light and Morse code

  • SOS flashlight on a big red button
  • Strobe, pulse, disco and Morse code in five speeds

Music: light to the beat

  • The flashlight and screen colours pulse to the music around you, offline

Timers and flash alerts

  • Turn the light off after a few minutes, or on at a set time
  • Flash alarm, and LED flash on calls and notifications

Private, with an optional Pro

The flashlight, SOS, screen light, patterns, timers and widgets are free forever. Better Torsh Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited Music, premium themes, the flash alarm and flash on notifications. No ads, no account, no analytics, and no internet permission: everything runs on your phone.
